About the Atlas

A plain map of AI tools, with the prices up front.

New AI tools launch every week, and each one hides its pricing behind a different page. The Atlas puts what a tool does, what you get free, and what it costs per month in one place, so you can pick before you pay.

Why it exists

Three questions, answered on one screen

Every card is built to answer the same three questions, in the same order, so tools are easy to compare.

What is it good for?

One plain sentence about what the tool is actually used for, not marketing language.

Can I try it free?

If there is a free plan, its limits are written out, such as credits per day or watermarks.

What does it cost?

Every paid plan with its monthly price in US dollars, so you can compare tiers at a glance.

Reading a card

What the colours and labels mean

Green is free. Orange is paid. The two are always kept apart, so you never mistake a trial for a plan you can keep.

Where prices come from

Checked against each tool's own pricing page

Prices are taken from each vendor's official pricing page, then reviewed before they go on the site. They change often, so every page shows the date of the last refresh.

If a price on the Atlas differs from what you see on the vendor's site, trust the vendor, and treat the Atlas as a good starting point.

Straight talk

What the Atlas does and does not do

What you can rely on

  • Links go straight to each tool's own site. There are no tracking or affiliate codes.
  • Free and paid are never mixed in the same box.
  • Prices are in US dollars per month unless a plan says otherwise.
  • The refresh date is always shown.

What to keep in mind

  • Prices move. Confirm on the vendor's site before you pay.
  • Taxes, regional pricing, yearly discounts and promotions are not all shown.
  • "Paid-only" means no free plan was found. The tool may still offer a trial.
  • The Atlas is independent and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any listed company.

Questions

Quick answers

Is the Atlas free to use?

Yes. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to pay.

Why is a tool I use missing?

The list is a curated snapshot, not every AI tool that exists. New tools and shutdowns are added by hand, so a missing tool may simply not have been added yet.

Why does a price here differ from the vendor's site?

Vendors change prices, credits and plan names often, and regional prices and taxes differ. The refresh date shows how recent the Atlas is, and the vendor's own page is always the final word.

What does "paid-only" mean?

No free plan was found for that tool. It may still offer a short trial or a money-back period, so check its site.

Why are some coding tools free but still cost money?

Open-source coding tools such as Cline and Aider are free to install, but they run on an AI model that you pay for by usage. Their cards say so.

Do the tool links earn money for the Atlas?

No. The links are plain addresses to each tool's own website.

The AI Tool Atlas is an independent directory. All product names belong to their owners. Always confirm current prices on the vendor's website before you pay.
